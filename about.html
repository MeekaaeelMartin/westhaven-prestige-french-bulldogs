About Westhaven

Breeding and showing French Bulldogs with a focus on champion lines, temperament, and lifelong commitment to the breed.

Who we are

Breeders & exhibitors

Westhaven is the home of Katrina and Colton West’s French Bulldog program — breeding and showing with care and ambition.

Their dogs include home-bred and carefully selected imports, with a current litter from dam Molina and external sire Kiro.

  • Family raised companions with show potential
  • Champion bloodlines and titled dogs
  • Transparent ownership — including clear notes when a sire is externally owned
